About BAID X

Ghana's work network, built on trust.

BAID X brings workers, companies, project managers, suppliers and clients onto one verified platform, so real projects get staffed, run and paid without the guesswork.

Explore BAID XRead the user guide →
The future of work in Africa isn't about connections. It's about trust.

Skilled people are everywhere. What's hard is knowing who to rely on, agreeing terms, and getting paid. BAID X puts identity checks, project tools, secure messaging and payments in one place so that good work and good people rise to the top.

Workers

Electricians, masons, plumbers, painters and more. Build a verified profile, find jobs, join projects and get paid.

Companies

Create projects, bring in a project manager and workers, and approve spending with full control.

Project managers

Run a company’s project day to day: team, tasks, reports and requests, within limits the company sets.

Suppliers

List materials and equipment, answer inquiries and win orders from real projects.

Individual clients

Hire a trusted professional for your home, and keep a record of who you’ve worked with.

How it works
Join and verifyCreate your account, complete your profile and submit your documents. A BAID X reviewer checks them and you earn the Verified badge.
ConnectDiscover verified people by trade and region. Message, call, invite, or apply to jobs.
Run the projectCompanies and project managers assign tasks, collect reports and approve requests. Workers update progress with photos.
Get paidPayments are requested, approved and recorded against the project. Earnings land in your wallet.
