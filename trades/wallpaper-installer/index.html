Questions people ask

How do I hire a verified wallpaper installer in Ghana?

Open BAID X, search for Wallpaper Installer and filter by your region. Check the badge on each profile, message to agree the scope and price, then hire. The payment is held in escrow until you approve the work.

Can I check a wallpaper installer's identity before hiring?

Yes. Profiles with a BAID X badge have had their Ghana Card reviewed, and higher badges add trade proof or a background check. The badge name tells you which checks were done.

How is a wallpaper installer paid on BAID X?

The client funds the job from their BAID X wallet, topped up through Paystack. BAID X holds the money while the work happens and releases it when the client approves, or automatically 3 days after the work is marked done if the client does not respond. Disputes freeze the money while BAID X reviews them. The worker withdraws to mobile money.

I am a wallpaper installer. How do I find work?

Create a free professional account, choose your trade and town, get verified, and apply for jobs or accept project invitations from companies and project managers.
