1Agreeing to these terms

By creating an account or using BAID X you agree to these terms. If you use BAID X for a company or organization, you confirm you have authority to bind it. If you don't agree, please don't use the service.

In shortBe honest, keep your account safe, treat people fairly, and pay for the work you agree to.

2Who can use BAID X

  • You must be at least 18 and able to enter a contract under Ghanaian law.
  • You must give accurate information and keep it current.
  • One person, one account. Companies may add staff through an organization.

3Roles and what they can do

BAID X has five account types: Worker, Company, Project manager, Supplier and Client. Each role sees different tools. You may only use the tools for your role.

  • Companies own projects, approve workers, spending and payments.
  • Project managers run a project for a company within the limits the company sets.
  • Workers apply for jobs, accept tasks and report progress.
  • Suppliers list products and equipment and answer inquiries.

4Verification and the badge

BAID X reviewers check identity and business documents. The Verified badge means we reviewed the documents you submitted at that time. It is not a guarantee of skill, quality or conduct.

  • Only BAID X decides verification. A paid plan never buys the badge.
  • We may remove the badge if information turns out to be false or out of date.

5Projects, work and payments

BAID X is a platform that connects people. Work agreements are between the people involved. We provide tools to record projects, tasks, reports, requests and payments.

  • Company approval is always required for expenses, materials, equipment and payments.
  • Payments are in Ghana cedis (GH₵). Mobile-money and card payments are processed by Paystack; we don't store card numbers.
  • Wallet, escrow, dispute and fee rules are set out in sections 6 to 9. Every fee is shown before you confirm.

6Wallet and payouts

6.1 What the wallet is. Your BAID X wallet is a record of money you've added to fund jobs, money held for jobs in progress, and money you've earned. It isn't a bank account. Balances don't earn interest, and the wallet can't be used for anything except activity on BAID X.

6.2 Adding money. You top up your wallet through Paystack, using mobile money or a card. Money shows as available once Paystack confirms the payment. We never see or store your full card details.

6.3 Where the money is held. Money in wallets and escrow is collected and paid out through our licensed payment partner, Paystack, and recorded separately from BAID X's own operating funds. We don't use your money to run the business.

6.4 Withdrawals. You can withdraw your available balance to a mobile money account (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash or AirtelTigo Money) registered in the same name as your verified BAID X profile. We don't pay out to third-party accounts. A person at BAID X checks each withdrawal before it is paid, and withdrawals usually arrive within 1 to 2 business days, but mobile networks and Paystack sometimes cause delays we can't control.

6.5 Holds and checks. We may delay or hold a withdrawal, or ask for extra information, if we suspect fraud, a stolen payment method, a chargeback, a legal requirement, or a breach of these terms. When we can, we'll tell you why and what we need from you.

6.6 Mistakes and chargebacks. If money reaches your wallet by mistake, or a top-up is later reversed by your bank or mobile money provider, we may deduct that amount from your wallet. If the balance isn't enough, you owe us the difference.

6.7 Closing your account. Withdraw your available balance before you close your account. We won't close an account while it still has money in escrow or an open dispute.

In shortYour wallet holds money you've added or earned. Top-ups go through Paystack, and withdrawals go to a mobile money account in your own name.

7Escrow: how job payments work

7.1 What escrow means on BAID X. When a client hires someone on BAID X, the agreed amount for the job or milestone moves from the client's wallet into escrow. BAID X holds it as a neutral party. The worker can see the job is funded, but nobody can use the money until it's released or refunded under these terms.

7.2 Who escrow applies to. In this section, the "client" is whoever is paying for the work (an individual client, a company, or a project manager acting within a company's limits). The "worker" is whoever is being paid (a professional, crew lead or supplier).

7.3 Agreeing the job. Before funding, the client and worker agree the scope, price, timeline and any milestones in BAID X. That record is what we'll rely on if there's a disagreement later, so make it clear. Changes to scope or price should be agreed in the app, not just verbally.

7.4 Milestones. A job can be split into milestones, each funded and released separately. Each milestone follows the same approval and auto-release rules, except that project milestones have a 5-day review window instead of 3 days.

7.5 Marking work done. When the work (or a milestone) is finished, the worker marks it done in the app and should attach proof, such as photos, a site report or a delivery note.

7.6 The 3-day review window. After the worker marks the work done, the client has 3 days (72 hours, counted from the time shown in the app, Ghana time) to do one of these:

  • Approve. The money is released to the worker.
  • Ask for changes. The worker can fix the work and mark it done again, which starts a new 3-day window.
  • Open a dispute. The money stays frozen (see section 8).

7.7 Automatic release. If the client does nothing within the 3-day window, the money is released to the worker automatically. The client is notified in the app when the work is marked done, and the release date is shown on the job.

7.8 Release is final. Once money is released, whether by approval or automatically, BAID X can't reverse it, except where the law requires it or we find fraud. Only approve work you're satisfied with.

7.9 Cancelling before work starts. If both sides agree to cancel before work starts, or the worker hasn't accepted the job within 3 days, the client can cancel and the escrowed amount goes back to the client's wallet in full. No BAID X fee is charged on a refund.

7.10 Cancelling after work starts. Once work has started, escrowed money can only go back to the client if the worker agrees in the app or BAID X decides it in a dispute. If the client and worker agree a partial payment, BAID X will split the money as agreed.

7.11 If a worker doesn't show up. If a worker hasn't started within 2 days of the agreed start date and hasn't explained why in the app, the client can cancel and get a full refund to their wallet.

7.12 Pay only through BAID X. Escrow protects only payments made through BAID X. Money paid in cash, by direct mobile money transfer, or by any other route outside the platform isn't protected, and we can't help recover it. Asking to be paid outside BAID X for a job found on BAID X breaks these terms.

7.13 Materials and supplier orders. For orders of materials or equipment, "work done" means the goods have been delivered or collected. The buyer's 3-day review window starts when the supplier marks the order delivered. Rental equipment follows the rental terms shown in the listing.

7.14 What escrow doesn't do. Escrow controls when money moves. It doesn't mean BAID X guarantees the quality, safety or legality of the work. The work agreement is still between the client and the worker.

In shortThe client funds the job first. BAID X holds the money while the work is done. The worker is paid when the client approves, or automatically 3 days after the worker marks the job done if the client doesn't respond.

8Disputes

8.1 Talk first. Most problems can be fixed by talking. Use BAID X messages to try to sort it out before you open a dispute.

8.2 Who can open a dispute, and when. Either side can open a dispute for a funded job or milestone from the moment it's funded until the money is released. Once money has been released, you can't open a dispute in BAID X, but you can still report fraud.

8.3 How to open one. Open the job, tap Open a dispute, choose a reason, and explain what went wrong and what outcome you want. Common reasons include: work not done, work not done as agreed, poor quality, wrong or damaged goods, no-show, or a payment disagreement.

8.4 What happens to the money. As soon as a dispute is opened, the money for that job or milestone is frozen. Nobody can release, refund or withdraw it until the dispute is resolved. Automatic release is paused.

8.5 Evidence. Both sides have 5 days to respond and upload evidence: photos, videos, site reports, receipts, the agreed scope, and any relevant messages. Private messages on BAID X are end-to-end encrypted, so we can't read them unless you choose to share them as evidence. If one side doesn't respond in time, we may decide based on what we have.

8.6 Our review. A BAID X reviewer will look at the evidence and may ask either side questions. We may ask for an inspection, or for photos of the site taken on a particular date. We aim to decide within 10 business days of receiving both sides' evidence. Complex jobs may take longer, and we'll tell you if they do.

8.7 Possible outcomes. We may:

  • release all the money to the worker,
  • refund all the money to the client's wallet,
  • split the money between them in a way we think is fair given the work completed, or
  • give the worker a set time to fix the work, then release or refund depending on the result.

8.8 Our decision. Our decision settles how the escrowed money is released on BAID X, and we'll carry it out. It's a platform decision, not a court judgment, and it doesn't stop either side from taking the matter to mediation, arbitration or the courts of Ghana. If a court or arbitrator later decides differently, the parties must settle the difference between themselves.

8.9 Fair use. Opening disputes in bad faith (for example, to delay a payment for work you've received, or to pressure someone into a lower price) breaks these terms. It may lead to account limits, suspension, or the loss of your verification badge. The same applies to false evidence.

8.10 Fees in a dispute. There's no charge to open a dispute or for our review, whatever the outcome. The service fee in section 9 applies only to the amount actually released to the worker.

8.11 Chargebacks. If you dispute a top-up with your bank, card issuer or mobile money provider instead of using BAID X disputes, we may freeze your wallet while the chargeback is investigated.

In shortIf something goes wrong, open a dispute before the money is released. The money stays frozen while BAID X looks at the evidence from both sides and decides how to release it.
