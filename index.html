Questions

Good to know.

Is BAID X free?

Yes. Every account type can join on the free Access plan and use the profile, search, messaging, applications and reviews. Paid plans are optional.

How does escrow protect me?

When you hire, you pay into escrow through Paystack. The worker can see the job is funded, but the money is only released when you approve the work or a milestone.

What does a badge mean?

A badge means BAID X reviewed that person’s documents: their profile, their ID, their trade proof or a full background check, depending on the badge. Badges cannot be bought.

How do workers get paid?

Released escrow goes into the worker’s BAID X wallet in Ghana cedis, from where it can be withdrawn to mobile money.

Are my messages private?

Chats are end-to-end encrypted, so only the people in the conversation can read them.

Is there an app?

Yes. BAID X runs in any browser and can be installed on your phone, and an Android app is on the way.
