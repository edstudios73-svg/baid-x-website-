Questions

Good to know.

Is BAID X free?

Yes. Every account type joins free on the Access plan. Optional Pro plans start at GH₵30 a month for workers. See the pricing page for every plan and fee.

How does escrow protect me?

When you hire, the job is funded from your BAID X wallet, which you top up through Paystack. BAID X holds the money while the work happens and releases it when you approve, or automatically 3 days after the worker marks it done if you do not respond. Disputes freeze the money while BAID X reviews them.

What does a badge mean?

A badge means a BAID X reviewer checked that person’s profile, ID, trade proof or background, depending on the badge. Checks carry a small review fee, which pays for the review, not the result. A paid plan never buys a badge.

Where does BAID X work?

In all 16 regions of Ghana. Amounts are in Ghana cedis and payouts go to mobile money.
