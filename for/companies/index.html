Staff, run and pay for your projects with people you can verify

BAID X gives construction and service companies one place to find verified workers and project managers, assign tasks, review reports and approve every payment.

What you get

Verified workforce

Search verified professionals and project managers by trade and region.

Project control

Create projects with a budget, team size and trades needed. Each gets a project ID.

Approval rules

Choose whether workers your project manager invites join automatically or need your approval. Money always needs your approval.

Reports & tasks

See daily and weekly reports, task progress and photos in one place.

Payments & records

Approve requests and record worker and manager payments against each project.

Organizations

Give your finance, site and admin staff the right level of access.
